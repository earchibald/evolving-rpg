# First Blood, Still Silent

> Sixty-five turns of empty floor, then a fight that killed in four blows — and still not one word out loud.

*world main · ended dead at turn 65, depth 1 · submitted: begin-again · 1 take(s), spoke: false*

## findings

- **Third run submitted, third run with the mic open and nothing on it.** The index shows the shape: 128 turns unwounded, then 6 turns wiped, now 65 turns and a death. Not one spoken word across any of them — not at the first blow, not at 0 hit points on turn 65. This is not a player who is bored into quitting; they keep coming back. It is a player who has not yet been given anything that costs them a breath. Nothing crossed the threshold of remark, including dying.

- **The one rule in this world is unfireable by construction, and it never fired.** The player held still 6 times and healed 0. The rule wants *nothing living within 6 squares* **and** *health below 99%* — but the only source of harm at depth 1 is a creature standing next to you, its pursuit is scent-driven with no leash (only the warden gets `VIGIL_LEASH`), and 3 damage a blow against 10 hit points means you die in four exchanges. You cannot be wounded and alone. Lens #61 puts every wound at 98% of the run, which means all 6 waits happened at full health, before the condition could even be true. Result: the world's only authored sentence — *"The breath you do not spend comes back to you."* — has never been printed. **The world has never spoken to this player.**

- **62 steps, 0 walls, 0 pickups — the walking is transit, not decision.** Forty-five turns went by with nothing about the situation changing. A floor you cross without once being obstructed and without once finding anything is a floor with no geometry and no reward gradient. There is no route to choose because all routes are the same route.

- **The fight is one decision made six times, and it was lost before turn one.** Six swings, no target choice, no positioning, no item, no ranged option in hand despite `SHOT_RANGE` existing in the tables. Two hits for 4 each, four blows taken for 3 each. The exchange rate was arithmetic from the first contact, and the player had no lever to change it. Death by subtraction is legible, but it is not a decision — there was no moment where a different input produced a different ending.

- **Lens #2 is reading the bounded-accuracy band working as designed, and that is the problem this early.** `NEEDED_FLOOR 4` / `NEEDED_CEILING 17` guarantees no roll sits outside expectation, and `critFloor(wits 3)` is a flat natural 20. Over 18 modelled outcomes, no nat-20 came. The design's own "5% of blows become stories" tooth produced zero stories, so every roll in this run was noise with no tail on either side.

## recommendations

1. **Smallest: change one number in the rest rule's radius, 6 → 3.** The rule reuses `CALL_RANGE` (6) as its quiet radius; `LURK_RANGE` (3) is the distance a player can actually open by walking. Forge candidate — *trigger:* on-wait; *conditions:* nothing living within **3** squares, health below 99%; *effects:* heal 1, say "The breath you do not spend comes back to you." Same rule, same words, but now reachable after a fight instead of never.

2. **Give the rule a denial line — fiction, in the wait/hold-still prompt.** When the player waits and the condition fails, the world should say which half failed: *"It is still watching. The breath will not come back to you."* / *"Nothing here is spent."* Six waits produced six blank turns; the machinery is invisible, so the player cannot learn the rule exists, let alone play toward it. One sentence converts dead air into a taught mechanic.

3. **Tuning, in `src/core/tables.ts`: depth-1 contact and loot placement, not to-hit.** The 60% anchor at `neededToHit(might 3, speed 2) = 9` is right; leave it. The number that failed is *when the floor first touches you* — 45 turns of nothing, then everything. Move depth-1 placement so first contact lands inside the first ~15 turns, and so at least one `ARMORY` or `PROVISIONS` entry sits on the traveled path. Sixty-two steps returning zero objects is the loot table never being consulted, not bad luck.

4. **The one big swing: put a second verb in the player's hand before the first fight.** The slinger exists in the code; the player fought with one input. Seed a ranged relic or a provision on depth 1 within reach, so the six-swing melee becomes a choice between closing, holding, and drawing. That is the difference between "I lost the arithmetic" and "I chose wrong" — and a wrong choice is the first thing in this game worth saying out loud.

## the shape of it

It is getting more fun, barely and for the first time: two runs ago the world could not wound this player, and now it can kill them. First blood is real progress and should not be undersold. But the run still contains exactly one event, it arrives at 98%, and it is unsurvivable by design rather than by play. The single absence that matters most is not difficulty and not content — it is **that the world has never once spoken**. One rule is in force, it is structurally impossible to satisfy, and its sentence has never printed. Until the game answers something the player did with words of its own, the player has no reason to answer back, and the microphone will keep coming home empty.
