- **The chain says 0 pickups. The player says pickup broke.** Both cannot be complete. The player reached for something — that is what "encountered" means — and the chain has no event for it. The mechanism is an unlogged failure path: whatever branch handles *swap* and *no-better-than* returns without emitting anything the chain can see. Every lens, every later reading, and every rule that might one day trigger on it are all blind to the exact moment the player noticed the game was wrong.

At the moment of friction they switched channels — out of the in-world voice and into the out-of-world bug box — and then they didn't come back to finish the floor. That switch is the finding. ## recommendations

- **Smallest: emit a chain event for the refused pickup.** The refusal path — swap declined, item no-better-than-held — should log the same way a successful pickup does, with the item, the held item, and the reason. One event. Until that exists, this bug is unreproducible from the chain and every future report of it will be a typed note with no facts behind it.

- **Gate the lens prose on sample size.** In the lens confidence table, give #61 (Interest Curve) a floor of turns and #2 (Surprise) a floor of modelled outcomes; below the floor, emit *no reading* rather than a reading plus a disclaimer. A lens that speaks over an empty sample teaches readers to discount it when it's right.

- **Forge candidate — make the refusal speak, matching the walk-over refusal already in force.** Trigger: attempted pickup. Condition: hands full **and** candidate is not better than what is held. Effect: journal line, no state change, no turn consumed. The full-hands walk-over case already has a voice; the no-better-than case appears to be its silent twin, and silence is exactly what the player read as a bug. Confirm against the pickup handler before forging — the typed note may describe a genuine state corruption rather than a missing message, and if so this rule papers over it instead of fixing it.

- **Fiction prompt: write the weighing line.** The refusal needs a sentence in the world's voice — the character considering the thing on the ground against the thing in hand and choosing to keep what they have. One line, generated at pickup-refusal, so the player learns the rule by being told it rather than by filing a report.
