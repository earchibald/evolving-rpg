# BONES — what finding your body should confer

**DECIDED 2026-07-27** (the designer delegated the call; the choice below
follows §2's own analysis and a second research pass on graves and loss
aversion). The composition ships as:

- **D stands as the floor** — finding your body always narrates.
- **Information, never stats**: standing where you fell borrows that dead
  life's *eyes* — the floor as it explored it joins your map (pure
  event-sourcing: the dead run's seen-tiles were always on its timeline).
  Zero exploit surface; the tradition's hard rule ("emotionally hot,
  mechanically cold") kept exactly.
- **F stays open** — `bodyHere` is in the rule vocabulary, so any world may
  yet ratify its own meaning for its dead, through the trials like any law.
- **B arrives once, at the bottom** — during the ninth floor's holdout, the
  world's first stir raises an echo from every body on that floor, wearing
  your current strength whole. Your deepest deaths become the last things
  between you and out. Depth-9 only, once per run: no farming surface.
- Bodies are never lootable. NetHack needed an 80% curse tax because it
  chose lootable corpses; we decline the whole branch.

The survey that fed the decision follows, unchanged.

The designer's call to make, prepared: the tradition surveyed (decades of
it), the option space distilled, nothing chosen. Written 2026-07-26.
What exists today: your body persists in this world's grave timelines; the
same seed rebuilds the same floors, so the place means the place; stepping
where you fell says *"you find your own body. what that is worth, the world
has not yet decided."* Messaging only — this document is the menu for the
rest.

## 1. What the games did

| Game / family | What persists | Finding it confers | The risk attached |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NetHack bones** (since Hack 1.0, 1984) | the level as it stood: corpse, inventory, a ghost at your former strength | the whole former inventory — often above your tier | ~80% of items cursed; the ghost fights back; ~1-in-3 chance a bones level even loads |
| **Shiren / Mystery Dungeon** | nothing, unless rescued | a second party can walk to your death tile and revive you — run resumes intact | the rescuer can die too; both parties' items gone |
| **Dark Souls bloodstain** | one stain holding all souls lost | touch it: 100% back. Others see a 10s replay of your death | die again first and it is overwritten — total forfeit, no partial credit |
| **Diablo 1/2 corpse** | everything worn, at the spot | click to re-equip | gold gone outright; D1 let others loot you; D3 cut it as "tedious, not tense" |
| **ZAngband player ghosts** | a text record of the fallen | nothing — the record spawns as a hostile unique themed after them | the encounter itself |
| **Hollow Knight shade** | a hostile mirror of you holding your currency | kill it: currency and full soul back | die first and the old shade (and its holdings) are deleted |
| **Minecraft / Terraria** | inventory spilled at the spot (by difficulty tier) | pick it up | 5-minute despawn, lava, thieves; Terraria tiers the harshness |
| **Spelunky** | **nothing, on purpose** | nothing | the purest permadeath position: no echo, no consolation |
| **Death Stranding** | a permanent crater where you died | nothing — the world is scarred, and heals slowly | the deaths that made it empowered what killed you |

The synthesis the whole tradition agrees on — why bones delight:
**danger** (losable forever), **reward that is only what you already earned**
(recovery reads as undoing a mistake, not farming), **continuity** (the
world remembers *you*: your name on the ghost, your gear in the pile, your
death replayed for a stranger).

## 2. The options, fitted to this game

| Option | Tradition | Here, concretely | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Recover gear** | NetHack, Diablo, Minecraft | the body carries what you wore; standing on it takes the better pieces (slots already handle replace-not-stack) | event sourcing gives the death snapshot free; risk ties to itemization | retrieval can become a chore (Diablo 3's stated reason for cutting it) |
| **B. Fight your ghost** | NetHack ghost, ZAngband, Hollow Knight | the fallen you spawns hostile at its dying stats/gear when first seen — architecturally poetic here: the ghost is a *replay of a logged character* | death becomes a boss-check scaled to your own former power | balance burden; needs care so it is neither trivial nor a wall |
| **C. One-shot recovery** | Dark Souls | the body holds your lost XP-above-level; reach it before dying again or it is gone | the tightest tension loop; cheap to build | double-punishes under a permadeath-lite premise; all-or-nothing |
| **D. Narrative only** (today) | Death Stranding, ADOM, Spelunky-adjacent | the line that already fires; maybe the gamemaster remembers your deaths | zero exploit surface; stacks under any other option | may never satisfy "should confer *something*" |
| **E. Corpse as resource** | Lost Souls MUD, corpse-eating | harvest the body for a consumable | feeds a future economy | tonally cheap here; invites death-farming; no economy exists yet |
| **F. The Forge decides** | *ours alone* | death proposals may offer body-rules ("when you stand where you fell → …"), trialled and ratified like any rule — the meaning of your body becomes something the world and you negotiate, per world | the most on-premise answer this game can give; different worlds could ratify different meanings | unpredictable; needs the body exposed to the rule vocabulary (a `bodyHere` condition) first |

Options compose: **D is already the floor**; A/B/C are classic single
answers; **F is the game being itself** — and B-via-F (a world that ratifies
its own ghosts) is the kind of sentence this project exists to make true.

## 3. Knobs, whatever is chosen

- Frequency gate (NetHack loads bones ~1-in-3 eligible; ours could gate by
  depth or per world).
- Curse/tax on recovery (NetHack's 80% curse; ours could dent grants).
- Overwrite policy (Souls: one live body; ours keeps every grave timeline —
  surfacing only the most recent avoids body-litter).
- Bots/assay: any mechanical option must face the trials (a body that
  confers gear is a greed-trial subject; a ghost is a coward-trial one).
